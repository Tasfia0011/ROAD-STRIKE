1. OVERVIEW OF ALL UPDATES & IMPLEMENTATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
In mainaai.py, a complete 3D Car driving simulator system was added without 
affecting existing world generation, lighting, sky cycle, rain, or building grid.
All implementations strictly use PyOpenGL, GLU, and GLUT features found in the 
existing repository files (main.py, # ====== PyOpenGL Multiple Bouncing Ball.py, 
3D_OpenGL_Intro.py, Hello_openGL.py, and Lets_draw_sth.py).

Key Features Implemented:
1. 3D Car Model (draw_3d_car):
   - Detailed 3D car model with glossy red chassis, lower skirts, headlights, 
     taillights, side mirrors, and 4 wheel cylinders with rim caps.
   - Car glass was removed to create an open cabin frame.
   - 3D Driver (Player Character) added sitting in the driver seat, complete with 
     torso/jacket, head sphere, cap, arms, and an interior 3D steering wheel.
   - Oriented correctly facing forward (-Z direction).

2. Camera System & Dual Mode Switching ('V' Key):
   - Toggles between 3rd Person View ("3rd") and 1st Person View ("1st") when 'V' 
     or 'v' is pressed.
   - 3rd Person View: Camera is positioned behind the car (car_x, 6.0, car_z + 16.0) 
     looking forward (car_x, 1.5, car_z - 10.0). When steering left or right, 
     the camera moves left/right together with the car, staying fixed to the rear.
   - 1st Person View: Camera positioned inside the cockpit facing forward over the hood, 
     overlaying the 2D Simulator Dashboard.

3. Vehicle Controls (WASD & 4 Arrow Keys):
   - W / UP Arrow: Accelerates car forward up to max speed (300 km/h).
   - S / DOWN Arrow: Brakes / Reverses car.
   - A / LEFT Arrow: Steers car left.
   - D / RIGHT Arrow: Steers car right.

4. Simulator Dashboard Overlay (draw_dashboard):
   - Speedometer Dial: 0 to 300 km/h scale with exactly 16 scale numbers 
     (0, 20, 40, 60, 80, 100, 120, 140, 160, 180, 200, 220, 240, 260, 280, 300).
   - Working RPM Meter: 0 to 8 (x1000 RPM) scale with redline section. Needle reacts 
     dynamically to acceleration and engine revs.
   - Simple Classic Aesthetics: Solid black circle backplates with yellow borders, 
     all scale numbers written in yellow text, and bright red indicator needles.
   - Temperature gauge removed as requested.
   - Center Digital Cluster: Speed readout (KM/H), gear mode (D/R), odometer (165376), 
     glowing turn arrows (<-- / -->), fuel gauge, and rotating steering wheel.

5. 100% Solid Pitch-Black Asphalt Road (draw_roads):
   - GL_FOG is temporarily disabled during road drawing (glDisable(GL_FOG)) so the 
     light blue sky fog never washes out or tints the road color, guaranteeing 
     pure pitch-black asphalt (0.01, 0.01, 0.01).

6. Butter-Smooth 60+ FPS Performance:
   - Optimized road render radius (ROAD_DRAW_RADIUS = 2) and lane marking step loops 
     to eliminate render bottlenecks and deliver smooth 60+ FPS gameplay.

2. DETAILED CODE EXPLANATION (BY FUNCTION / BLOCK)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A. Global Variables & Configurations
----------------------------------
- car_x, car_z = 0.0, 0.0
  Tracks the 3D position of the car in the world space.
- cam_x, cam_z = 0.0, 15.0
  Tracks the camera position in world space.
- car_speed = 0.0
  Current speed of the vehicle in km/h (0 to 300 km/h).
- max_speed = 300.0
  Maximum forward speed limit.
- current_rpm = 900.0
  Current engine RPM (idle 900 RPM up to 8000 RPM redline).
- camera_mode = "3rd"
  Current view mode ("3rd" for 3rd Person View, "1st" for 1st Person FPV).
- key_states = {'w': False, 's': False, 'a': False, 'd': False, 'up': False, 'down': False, 'left': False, 'right': False}
  Tracks pressed state for WASD and 4 Arrow keys.


B. Input Handlers (keyboard_down, keyboard_up, special_down, special_up)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
- keyboard_down(key, x, y):
  Detects ASCII key presses. If 'v' or 'V' is pressed, toggles camera_mode between 
  "1st" and "3rd". Stores pressed state in key_states.
- special_down(key, x, y) & special_up(key, x, y):
  Uses GLUT special key constants (GLUT_KEY_UP, GLUT_KEY_DOWN, GLUT_KEY_LEFT, 
  GLUT_KEY_RIGHT) to track 4 Arrow key presses smoothly.


C. Vehicle Physics Engine (update_vehicle_physics)
--------------------------------------------------
- Acceleration & Braking:
  When throttle ('w' or 'up') is pressed, increases car_speed by acceleration * dt.
  When brake/reverse ('s' or 'down') is pressed, decreases car_speed.
  When no drive keys are pressed, coasting deceleration gradually slows speed to 0.
  Clamps speed between max_reverse_speed (-60 km/h) and max_speed (300 km/h).
- Sideways Car Movement:
  When steer left ('a' or 'left') is pressed, car_x decreases.
  When steer right ('d' or 'right') is pressed, car_x increases.
  Clamps car_x between -35.0 and +35.0 to stay within road boundaries.
- Forward Movement Translation:
  Translates car_z along -Z by (car_speed * 0.25 * dt).
  Updates cam_x = car_x and cam_z = car_z + 16.0 so the camera follows behind.
- Engine RPM Calculation:
  Calculates speed_ratio = car_speed / 300.0. Simulates multi-gear engine rev cycles 
  and throttle boost, smoothly updating current_rpm towards target_rpm (900 to 8000 RPM).


D. 3D Car & Driver Renderer (draw_3d_car)
-----------------------------------------
- Uses glPushMatrix(), glTranslatef(cx, 0.2, cz), glRotatef(angle, 0, 1, 0) to 
  position and orient the car.
- Chassis & Body: Drawn using draw_box() helper function.
- Open Cabin & Pillars: Draws top windshield bar and side door frame pillars (glass 
  boxes removed).
- 3D Driver Character:
  - Driver Torso: Box drawn in blue sports jacket at (-0.55, 1.05, 0.1).
  - Driver Head: Skin tone sphere using gluSphere(quad, 0.32, 10, 10).
  - Driver Cap: Dark cap sphere drawn above head.
  - Driver Arms: Skin tone boxes extending forward to holding position.
  - Interior 3D Steering Wheel: Cylinder drawn inside cabin in front of driver.
- Lights & Wheels: Front headlights placed at -Z (glowing white), rear taillights 
  placed at +Z (glowing red), and 4 wheel cylinders drawn with metallic rim caps.


E. Simulator Dashboard Overlay (draw_dashboard)
-----------------------------------------------
- Switches matrix mode to GL_PROJECTION, loads identity, and sets 2D orthographic 
  view using gluOrtho2D(0, WINDOW_WIDTH, 0, WINDOW_HEIGHT).
- Disables GL_DEPTH_TEST and GL_FOG for clean 2D HUD rendering.
- Dashboard Base: Draws dark black quad base across bottom window area.
- Speedometer (Left Dial):
  - Black background circle drawn with draw_hud_circle(..., r=0.0, g=0.0, b=0.0).
  - Yellow border ring and 16 yellow tick marks.
  - 16 Scale Numbers (0 to 300, gap of 20) rendered around arc in YELLOW text 
    using glutBitmapCharacter(GLUT_BITMAP_HELVETICA_18, ...).
  - Speed Needle: Angle calculated via sp_ratio = speed / 300.0. Pointer line 
    drawn in bright RED (1.0, 0.0, 0.0) from dial center to arc edge using sin/cos.
- Tachometer / Working RPM Meter (Right Dial):
  - Black background circle with yellow border outline.
  - Scale numbers 0 to 8 (x1000 RPM) rendered in YELLOW text.
  - RPM Needle: Angle calculated via rpm_ratio = current_rpm / 8000.0. Pointer line 
    drawn in bright RED (1.0, 0.0, 0.0) reacting to engine revs.
- Center Cluster & Indicators:
  - Displays digital speed (KM/H), gear indicator (D/R), odometer, glowing yellow 
    turn arrows (<-- / -->), fuel gauge, and an interactive 2D steering wheel 
    that rotates when turning left/right.


F. Pitch-Black Road Renderer (draw_roads)
-----------------------------------------
- Wraps road rendering in glDisable(GL_FOG) ... glEnable(GL_FOG).
- Sets asphalt color to set_env_color(0.01, 0.01, 0.01).
- Disabling fog during road drawing prevents sky fog from tinting the road, 
  ensuring 100% solid pitch-black asphalt.


G. Main Display Callback (display)
----------------------------------
- Clears color & depth buffers (glClear).
- Configures camera perspective using gluLookAt:
  - 3rd Person View: gluLookAt(car_x, 6.0, car_z + 16.0, car_x, 1.5, car_z - 10.0, 0, 1, 0)
  - 1st Person View: gluLookAt(car_x, 2.2, car_z, car_x, 2.0, car_z - 20.0, 0, 1, 0)
- Streams world grid around car position (stream_world(car_x, car_z)).
- Renders ground, footpaths, roads, world buildings/trees/lamps, rain, 3D car model, 
  and 2D simulator dashboard (when in 1st Person View).
- Swaps buffers with glutSwapBuffers().
